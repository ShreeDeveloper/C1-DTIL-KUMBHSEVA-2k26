Crowd safety

Demo status: Information only

Crowd conditions are not connected to a live control room.

  • Follow official police and organiser instructions.
  • Do not push; move with the crowd and keep children close.
  • Agree on a meeting point with your group.
  • If an area is blocked, do not cross barricades; follow signed diversions.
  • Keep drinking water and essential medicines with you.

Emergency & lost-person help

🚨 Immediate danger

Use verified local emergency services or approach uniformed police/official help desks. Do not rely on this student prototype for emergency dispatch.

Call India emergency number 112
